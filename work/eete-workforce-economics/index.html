The full method applied to one domain: costs, scenarios and trade-offs laid out with their evidence, and the decision left with you.

I The problem

Employment is one of the largest economic agreements most people and firms make, yet it is priced and judged with little transparency on either side.

II Approach

  1. Treat the employee as a service provider and the employment contract as a service agreement with measurable terms.
  2. Analyse the economics of a role from both sides: what it costs, what it returns and how fairly it is priced.
  3. Start with concierge-style pilots for recruiters and enterprise HR and rewards teams before building the full platform.
  4. Turn each analysis into a decision a recruiter, employer or employee can explain and defend.

III Structure

  1. InputsRole, contract and market data
  2. AnalysisCost, value and fairness of the employment agreement
  3. DecisionA defensible recommendation with its evidence
  4. PlatformCareer economics decision intelligence, after the pilots
