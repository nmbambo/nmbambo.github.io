A worked decision record

One important decision, shown with its working: the question, the authority, the evidence, the assumptions, the options, the decision, the conditions for execution and the criteria for review.

Status
In preparation
Nature
Illustrative; no client data and no client results

I What it will show

  1. QuestionWhat is being decided, and what is not.
  2. AuthorityWho holds the decision, and within what limits.
  3. EvidenceWhat was known, with its source and its pedigree.
  4. AssumptionsWhat was taken as given, and what would count against it.
  5. OptionsThe alternatives weighed, including doing nothing.
  6. DecisionThe choice, its rationale, and the named person who made it.
  7. Execution conditionsWhat must hold for the decision to be carried out faithfully.
  8. Review criteriaWhen it is reviewed, and how anyone will know whether it worked.

The record is being written now. Until it is published, the Decision Canvas and Governance Standard shows the fields every Ingqiqo decision record carries.
