· What it gives you

If you want your reports, systems and teams to stop disagreeing about what a customer, a sale or a risk is,

One governed meaning, defined once and used everywhere, with a trace from every figure back to the definition it relied on.

I The problem

Organisations hold the same idea in a dozen shapes: a column in one system, a policy clause in another, a judgement in a meeting. Each shape drifts. Decisions built on drifting meaning cannot be trusted or explained.

II Approach

  1. Start from a Meaning Foundation that runs from terminology to taxonomy, ontology, semantics and context.
  2. Express that meaning through three peers: data as evidence, process as execution, and decision as commitment.
  3. Govern all three through a contract and assurance plane built on four disciplines: integrity, validity, conscience and optimisation.
  4. Compose the assessments of each discipline in a coherence engine that says whether a decision still means what it claims.

III Structure

  1. Meaning FoundationTerminology, taxonomy, ontology, semantics, context
  2. ExpressionsData and evidence · Process and execution · Decision and commitment
  3. Assurance planeIntegrity · Validity · Conscience · Optimisation
  4. Coherence engineComposes runtime assessments into one verdict
