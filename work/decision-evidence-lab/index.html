Decision Evidence Lab

Testing whether the evidence behind a decision actually supports it.

Status
In preparation
Discipline
Scientist
Repository
Ingqiqo/decision-evidence-lab · opening soon

I The problem

Many decisions cite evidence that does not bear the weight placed on it. The gap is rarely dishonesty; it is untested inference.

II Approach

  1. State each decision as a claim with the evidence offered for it.
  2. Rate the evidence on a clear ladder, from anecdote to tested causal finding.
  3. Run the cheapest test that could prove the claim wrong.
  4. Record the outcome so the next decision learns from this one.

III Structure

  1. ClaimWhat the decision asserts
  2. EvidenceWhat is offered, rated on a ladder
  3. TestThe cheapest falsifying check
  4. LearningOutcome recorded against the claim
