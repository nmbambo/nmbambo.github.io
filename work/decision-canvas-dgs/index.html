I The problem

When a dispute, an audit or a regulator arrives, most organisations cannot show who decided, on what information, under what authority, or when the decision was due for review.

II Approach

  1. Record every significant decision on the Decision Canvas: twelve fields, in four lighter variants for smaller decisions.
  2. Review decision governance against the Decision Governance Standard: seventeen principles in four pillars.
  3. Score each pillar and produce a remediation plan a board can track.
  4. Keep people deciding: the canvas records the decision; it never makes it.

III Structure

  1. CanvasOwner, alternatives, evidence, authority, review date and more
  2. VariantsFour lighter forms for everyday decisions
  3. StandardSeventeen principles in four pillars
  4. RemediationA DGS score by pillar and a plan

IV Craft and next

Decision governance · Facilitation · Board reporting

The canvas is the human-scale form of the Decision IR: the same fields, before any platform is needed. It complements King IV and legal requirements; it does not replace them.
