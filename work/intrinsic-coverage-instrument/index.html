· What it gives you

If you want the people carrying a decision to be moved by it, not merely told,

A view of which intrinsic drivers the decision design covers and which it misses, checked again after execution, without ever scoring an individual.

I The problem

Decisions are designed around incentives and compliance, then fail in execution because the people asked to deliver them were never moved by them. Intrinsic motivation is treated as culture, not as a design input.

II Approach

  1. Before a managed decision, elicit twelve intrinsic drivers from the people who will carry it: meaning, choice, competence, ability, progress, growth, values alignment, identity, impact, contribution, consideration and autonomy.
  2. Have the facilitator assess, with evidence, how far the decision design covers each driver.
  3. After execution, pulse-check whether the coverage was realised, and flag gaps where importance was high and coverage low.
  4. Report coverage, never individuals: a matrix for the decision contract and a one-page coverage certificate.

III Structure

  1. ElicitTwelve drivers, rated and prioritised by the actors
  2. CoverFacilitator's evidence-based coverage assessment
  3. PulsePost-decision check on what was realised
  4. CertifyCoverage matrix and one-page certificate
