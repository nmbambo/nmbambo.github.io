· What it gives you

If you want analytics and operations to run on the same truth, not two copies that drift,

A lakehouse for evidence and a lakebase for action, joined by master data, so what you analyse is what you act on.

I The problem

Most data platforms move bytes well and meaning badly. By the gold layer nobody can say which definition a number used or who changed it, and the operational systems that act on the numbers disagree about who a customer even is.

II Approach

  1. Run the lakehouse leg on DuckLake as a data mesh: each domain, modelled with domain-driven design, owns its Bronze-to-Gold slice, and a logical data fabric virtualises access across domains.
  2. Land Bronze raw and immutable; model Silver as Data Vault 2.0; compile Gold into multi-format, poly-database projections from ledger tables: general, relationship, causal and event graphs, time series and dimensional models.
  3. Ingest with dlt and Airbyte, stream with Arroyo, extract documents with Unstructured.io, transform with dbt on DuckDB, and gate every tier with ODCS data contracts, Soda Core, YData Profiling, Pydantic v2 and pytest.
  4. Bridge to the lakebase leg, Postgres for applications with Polars on the app side, through multi-domain master data: Zingg matching driven by ontology metadata into AtroCore, with DuckDB, dlt and Airbyte carrying reverse ETL.
  5. Share one semantic layer between DuckDB and Polars, expose lakehouse metadata to other engines through Apache XTable, and trace every hop in DataHub with OpenLineage.

III Structure

  1. BronzeRaw, immutable, enveloped with source, hash, contract and privacy class
  2. SilverData Vault 2.0: hubs, links and satellites, insert-only
  3. GoldPoly-DB projections: graphs, time series, dimensional, compiled not hand-written
  4. Master dataZingg into AtroCore, metadata-driven, multi-domain
  5. LakebasePostgres for applications, the decision ledger and workflow
