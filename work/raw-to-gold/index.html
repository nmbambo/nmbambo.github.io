Raw to Gold

A governed raw-to-gold data platform build, auditable end to end.

Status
Phase 1 in build
Discipline
Engineer
Repository
Ingqiqo/raw-to-gold · opening soon

I The problem

Most data platforms move bytes well and meaning badly. By the gold layer nobody can say which definition a number used, or who changed it.

II Approach

  1. Take one real client dataset from raw landing to a governed gold layer.
  2. Carry definitions, lineage and quality checks with the data at every stage, not in a separate document.
  3. Keep personal information in South African regions to satisfy POPIA by design.
  4. Make every gold table answer three questions: where it came from, what it means, and who may rely on it.

III Structure

  1. RawLanded as received, immutable
  2. BronzeTyped and conformed
  3. SilverCleansed, joined, definitions applied
  4. GoldDecision-ready, documented, quality-gated
