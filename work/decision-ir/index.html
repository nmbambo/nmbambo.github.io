Decision IR

Decision representation and accountability, made machine and human readable.

Status
Schema defined; ledger in build
Discipline
Architect
Repository
Ingqiqo/decision-ir · opening soon

I The problem

Decisions are the most consequential artefacts an organisation produces, and the least recorded. Months later, nobody can show what was decided, on what evidence, or by whom.

II Approach

  1. Represent every decision in one canonical form that both people and machines can read.
  2. Link each decision to its evidence, its authority and its expected outcome.
  3. Evaluate it against the assurance disciplines before it is committed.
  4. Write it to a hash-chained ledger so the record cannot be quietly rewritten.

III Structure

  1. Decision IRCanonical decision representation
  2. Evidence linksWhat the decision rests on
  3. EvaluationIntegrity, validity, conscience, optimisation
  4. LedgerHash-chained, append-only record
