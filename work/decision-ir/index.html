Decision IR and the Warranty

Decisions compiled into one typed program, solved by a sovereign logic engine, recorded on a post-quantum ledger and warranted.

I The problem

Decisions are the most consequential artefacts an organisation produces, and the least recorded. Months later, nobody can show what was decided, on what evidence, by whom, or what was given up to get there.

II Approach

  1. Compile strategy, ontology terms, mastered identities, business rules and evidence into one typed, content-addressed Decision IR. Nothing bypasses it.
  2. Gate with GoRules, then solve on the Sovereign Logic Engine: weighted MaxSMT on Z3 over DPLL(T) and CDCL. The relaxation set, what was given up, returns with every answer.
  3. Write each decision to a hash-chained ledger: SHA3-256 links, ML-DSA signatures per entry, SLH-DSA checkpoints, with every entry recording its algorithms for crypto-agility.
  4. Trace outcomes back through the Motive, Ownership and Institutional Architecture models; the ICCD shows where culpability was manufactured by design and who the original mover was.
  5. Warrant process fidelity, not outcomes: the decision was made as specified, on the evidence recorded, by the authority named.

III Structure

  1. Decision IRCanonical, typed, content-addressed decision program
  2. Sovereign Logic EngineGoRules gating, weighted MaxSMT on Z3
  3. LedgerSHA3-256 chain, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA signatures
  4. AccountabilityMotive, Ownership, Institutional Architecture, ICCD
  5. Decision WarrantyProcess fidelity, evidenced by the ledger
