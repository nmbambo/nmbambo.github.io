The one-kick rule

Fear the person who has practised one kick ten thousand times, not the one who has practised ten thousand kicks once.

So the unit of learning here is not a chapter, a course or a certificate. It is a capability — a move you can perform. Every book, paper, tool, dataset and engagement is just new material on which to practise the same fourteen moves: frame, observe, decompose, model, challenge, perturb, compare, construct, destroy, repair, explain, execute, reflect, adapt.

Never study a concept without converting it into a move. Not "I know sensitivity analysis" but "when a decision rests on an assumption, I can find it, perturb it, locate the decision boundary and say how robust the choice is."

The victory condition

Become extraordinarily difficult to fool about decisions.

Not infallible. Not omniscient. Able to enter an unfamiliar system, find the decision hiding inside it, expose its assumptions, detect where strategy, intent, decision, execution and evidence stop lining up — and make it executable.

Completion gives access. A test gives recall. Building gives competence. Breaking and repairing give understanding. Pressure gives reliability. Teaching gives transfer. Discovery gives insight. Never confuse completion with mastery.
The centre of the Academy

Every discipline contributes a lens; no discipline owns the decision.

The spine is one loop — strategy → intent → decision → execution → outcome → evidence → feedback → strategy. Science gives knowledge, analysis understanding, architecture structure, engineering capability, optimisation better choices, adaptation resilience, strategy direction, coherence alignment, execution movement, law legitimate bounds, ethics normative bounds, executive management stewardship, feedback learning. We do not certify that you know Decision Science. We certify that you can make decision capability work.
