VI What stays where

This page keeps no server and no form service. Your details travel only in the email you send, the text you copy, and a private link inside it. Nothing is stored here, and nothing is sent until you press send in your own email app.

The page notes, in your browser only, that a request was made: its length and platform, never your name or words.
