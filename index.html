I Practice

One discipline, five ways of holding it.

Scientist

Frames the question before the answer. Tests whether evidence actually supports a decision, and says so when it does not.

Engineer

Builds the pipelines and platforms that carry meaning intact, from raw data to governed, auditable gold.

Analyst

Reads what the numbers are saying, and what they are hiding, then turns it into a choice someone can make.

Modeller

Gives terms, taxonomies and ontologies their shape, and models decisions so they can be represented, compared and reviewed.

Architect

Designs the whole: how data, process and decision fit together, and where accountability sits within them.
